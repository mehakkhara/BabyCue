Journal → Book and Reel

Two keepsake outputs built from what is already in the Journal. Nothing new to enter. Everything composes on the phone, like the keepsake cards: no uploads, no cost. Both would sit on the paid side with Browse by month.

Part 1 · The Book

AChapters, read in the app

A cover plus one chapter per month. Each chapter opens with the age and the month, a line pulled from one of her notes, then the photos as laid-out pages instead of a feed. Reads like a book on the phone, and "Save as PDF" prints it. The Journal tab stays as it is: the Book is a second way in.

AInside a chapter

The chapter opener: age line, the month as a title, one of her own notes set in italics as the epigraph. Then photo pages, two to four per page, each with its caption and the exact age. The age stamp is what turns a photo roll into a baby book.

BPrint-first: a PDF you order

Skip the in-app reading view. "Make my book" asks three things (which months, square or portrait, cover photo), then lays out a print-ready PDF in the browser and saves it to Files. From there she uploads it to any print service. Less to build, but the book only exists when she exports it.

Part 2 · The Reel

AMonth reel, narrated by her

All of one month's photos in about 30 seconds, vertical, each with a slow zoom and the age as a caption. The twist: her voice memos from that month play underneath. No stock music, no licensing, and it's her voice over her baby. Composed on a canvas, recorded to video on the phone, shared with the system share sheet.

BGrowth reel, one photo a month

The timelapse from the plan. One photo per month, cut fast, ending on today. Three seconds a month, so a year is twelve seconds and you watch them grow in one breath. Works best when the shot is similar each month, which the Photo hunt already encourages. Runs again automatically every month with the new frame on the end.

WhereEntry points
